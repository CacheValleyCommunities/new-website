## Event Overview

Everybody uses these models now. Far fewer of us could explain what's actually happening between typing a prompt and reading the response.

This month is an introduction to how large language models work, focused on the **GPT-2 architecture**. GPT-2 is a good place to start: it's open, it's small enough to reason about, and it's the same fundamental design that newer models scaled up.

## What We'll Cover

The terms that come up constantly and rarely get defined:

- What a token is
- What a parameter is
- What dimensions are
- What a transformer is

Expect to leave with a working mental model rather than a full derivation. Bring your questions, including the ones that feel too basic to ask.

## Who Should Attend?

All skill levels are welcome, and no prior machine learning experience is assumed. If you use AI tools and want a better sense of what's under the hood, this one is for you.
