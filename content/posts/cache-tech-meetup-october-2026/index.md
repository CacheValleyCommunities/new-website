### Event Details:

- **Date**: Saturday, October 24th, 2026
- **Time**: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM (MDT)
- **Location**: Logan Library, Makerspace, 285 N Main, Logan, UT 84321

## Event Overview

Join us for our October Cache Tech Community meetup and a **tour of the Logan Library's Cytiva STEM Makerspace**! We'll explore the space, learn about the tools available, and get ideas for future projects. Come connect with fellow tech enthusiasts from across Cache Valley and see what you could make here.

## What We'll Cover

The makerspace has equipment for everything from building physical objects to preserving old photos and recordings. Available tools include:

- **3D printing**: Bambu and mini 3D printers, plus a 3D printing pen.
- **Cutting and engraving**: An xTool laser cutter/engraver, a vinyl cutter, and a Cricut Explore 3.
- **Printing and custom designs**: Sublimation and large-format printers, heat presses, and a mug press.
- **Sewing and textiles**: Sewing and embroidery machines, a serger, and fabric-cutting tools.
- **Digitizing media**: A photo scanner, film converter, and equipment for working with cassettes, records, VHS tapes, and other audio and video.

Computers with design software and equipment drivers are also available. Bring your questions and project ideas!

See the library's [Cytiva STEM Makerspace page](https://library.loganutah.gov/about_us/services/makerspace.php) for the full equipment list, reservation links, and details about using the space.

## Who Should Attend?

All skill levels are welcome. Whether you're a local developer, a student, an educator, or simply curious about technology, we'd love to have you join us.
