Come join the Creative Support Studio in the Cytiva Makerspace on Thursdays from 6-8:30 PM (starting May 21) for a weekly drop-in workspace for creatives of all kinds! Teens need to be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Bring your own project to enjoy a structured environment with quiet work time, optional conversation breaks, and a supportive community. If you need ideas or some help getting started, optional guidance and short demos will be available.
